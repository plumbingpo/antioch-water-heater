Keeping Your Brentwood Water Heater Running Longer

Routine maintenance is the difference between a water heater that runs for years and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and yearly descaling for any tankless unit on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, tankless units often reach 20 with regular descaling, and heat pump models usually last 10 to 15. If your heater is past that window and showing symptoms such as discolored hot water, popping and rumbling, dampness under the tank, or showers that go cold sooner, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one with water on the floor.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or a missing expansion tank, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip ignored for months can keep stressing the tank until the tank itself fails, and a small repair becomes a full replacement plus water damage. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

What Goes Wrong With Local Water Heaters

Long before a tank floods a Brentwood garage, it almost always sends signals. Pay attention to a popping or rumbling sound while the tank heats, rust-colored hot water, a faint rotten-egg smell, a damp spot under the tank, or hot water that runs out a little sooner each month. These are early signs of heavy sediment, a depleted anode, a weak element or burner, and early corrosion, and caught early, most of them mean a modest repair or a replacement on your schedule. Ignored, they lead to a failed tank and water on the floor. Pay close attention to a relief valve that drips or discharges: it means the tank is seeing too much pressure or temperature, and it needs a professional look soon. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so the small problem never gets the chance to grow.
